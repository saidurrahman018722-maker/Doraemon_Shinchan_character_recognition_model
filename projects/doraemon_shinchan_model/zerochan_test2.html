Nobita Nobi(のび太野比)

He is the co-star of the series. Is lazy, clumsy and unlucky in life, besides being bad for the studies, is tormented by Takeshi Goda (also known as Giant) and Tsuneo. Doraemon always pays future inventions that were allegedly designed to solve serious problems, but Nobita just using them for their own enjoyment and the final plans do not go as he expects. Shizuka is in love and marry in the future. Strangely Nobita always breaks Shizuka while she is bathing.
